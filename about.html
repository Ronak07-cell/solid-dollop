About QuickCalc

QuickCalc is a free collection of simple, fast calculators built to answer the exact math questions people search for every day — no sign-up, no clutter, just a quick answer.

It started as a small personal project to build something genuinely useful while learning web development, and has grown into a small toolkit of calculators covering percentages, CGPA conversion, BMI, and age.

Every calculation happens instantly in your browser — nothing is sent to a server, and your calculation history is saved only on your own device.

Have a suggestion for a calculator you'd like to see? Visit the Contact page.

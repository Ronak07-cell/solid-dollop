What this site collects

QuickCalc's calculators save your calculation history directly in your browser using a technology called localStorage. This data stays on your device only — it is never sent to us or stored on any server. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

Cookies and advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use this site, such as which pages are viewed and how long visitors stay. This data is anonymized and used only to improve the site.

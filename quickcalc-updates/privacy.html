Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains what information QuickCalc (ronak07-cell.github.io) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you enter into calculators

Values you type into the calculators are processed in your browser. They are not sent to us or stored on any server. Your recent calculation history is saved locally on your device using the browser's localStorage feature. Clearing your browser data, or using the "Clear History" button, removes it.

Log and usage data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may collect technical information such as IP address, browser type and requested pages in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are viewed, approximate location, device type and how visitors arrive. Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers. To opt out, you can install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies, including advertising cookies, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to show ads based on your browsing.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform may appear asking you to accept or manage cookies used for advertising and measurement. You can change your choice at any time using the controls in that message.

Children's privacy

QuickCalc is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party links

The site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

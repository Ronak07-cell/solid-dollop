About QuickCalc

QuickCalc is a free collection of simple, fast calculators built to answer the exact math questions people search for every day — no sign-up, no clutter, just a quick answer.

Who builds it

QuickCalc is built and maintained by Ronak Dhaka, a software developer from India. It started as a personal project to build something genuinely useful while learning web development, and has grown into a small toolkit covering percentages, CGPA conversion, BMI and age.

Why these calculators

The tools were chosen because they are searched for constantly, and are often wrapped in cluttered pages. For students in India, the CGPA to percentage converter in particular answers a question that comes up on almost every application form.

How the calculators work

Every calculation runs instantly in your browser using standard, published formulas. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and your calculation history is saved only on your own device. Each calculator page explains the formula it uses, with worked examples, so you can check the result yourself.

Accuracy and corrections

Results are intended for general information. For official purposes such as admissions, medical decisions or legal eligibility, always confirm with the relevant institution or professional. If you spot a mistake, please tell us through the Contact page and it will be fixed.

How the site is funded

QuickCalc is free to use. It may show ads through Google AdSense to cover hosting and development time. Ads never change calculation results.

History

    How to convert CGPA to percentage

    CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) summarises your performance across all subjects on a 10-point scale. Many employers, colleges and application forms ask for a percentage instead, so you need to convert it. The calculator above does this in one step using the formula your institution follows.

    The two common formulas

    FormulaCalculationCommonly used by
    CBSE standardPercentage = CGPA × 9.5CBSE schools (Class 10)
    Simple multiplierPercentage = CGPA × 10Some universities and colleges

    CBSE uses 9.5 because it is based on the average relationship between grade points and marks across grade bands, so a straight ×10 would slightly overstate the marks. Always check which formula your own board or university officially accepts.

    Worked examples

    Example 1: CBSE student

    CGPA 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%.

    Example 2: University with the ×10 rule

    CGPA 7.6 × 10 = 76%.

    Example 3: Same CGPA, different result

    With a CGPA of 9.0, the CBSE formula gives 85.5% while the ×10 rule gives 90%. The 4.5-point gap is why choosing the correct formula matters for applications and scholarships.

    Converting percentage back to CGPA

    To reverse the conversion, divide by the same multiplier: CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5 (CBSE) or Percentage ÷ 10. For example, 85% ÷ 9.5 ≈ 8.95 CGPA.

    Tips for getting it right

    • Check the official circular or prospectus of the institution you are applying to, since some accept only their own conversion rule.
    • Some universities issue an official equivalence certificate. If you can get one, use it instead of calculating yourself.
    • For multi-semester degrees, use the final cumulative CGPA rather than a single semester's SGPA.
    • Keep two decimal places when the form asks for exact percentage, and do not round up beyond what the form allows.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is CGPA × 9.5 valid for university CGPA?

    Not always. It is the official CBSE rule, but universities set their own policies. Some adopt ×9.5, some ×10, and some have their own table.

    What is the difference between CGPA and SGPA?

    SGPA is the grade point average for one semester. CGPA is the cumulative average across all semesters so far.

    Does this calculator work for a 4-point or 5-point scale?

    No. It is built for the 10-point scale used in India. Other scales need a different conversion.

    How the age calculator works

    Enter a date of birth and the calculator tells you your exact age in years, months and days. It is useful for forms that ask for age on a specific date, eligibility checks for exams and jobs, school admissions, insurance, and simply finding out how old someone is today.

    How age is calculated

    Age is the difference between today's date and the date of birth, counted in whole calendar units:

    1. Subtract the birth year from the current year.
    2. If the birthday has not yet happened this year, subtract one year and add 12 months to the month difference.
    3. If the day of the month has not yet been reached, borrow one month and add the number of days in the previous month.

    Because months have different lengths (28 to 31 days), counting by calendar months is more accurate than dividing total days by 30.

    Worked examples

    Example 1

    Born 15 March 2000, today is 9 October 2026. Years: 26. Months: from 15 March to 15 September is 6 months, and 15 September to 9 October is 24 days. Age = 26 years, 6 months, 24 days.

    Example 2: Leap-year birthday

    Someone born on 29 February 2004 has a true birthday only in leap years. In other years most people celebrate on 28 February or 1 March, and legal systems differ on which counts, so check the rule for your use case.

    Age for exam and job eligibility

    Many Indian exams specify a cut-off date and a maximum or minimum age on that date, with relaxations for certain categories. To check yours, enter your date of birth and compare against the official notification's reference date, not today's date. Always rely on the official notice for final eligibility.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the calculator account for leap years?

    Yes. It uses actual calendar dates, so leap days are handled correctly.

    Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

    This version calculates age as of today. For another date, subtract your birth date from that date by hand using the steps above.

    Is my date of birth saved?

    Only in your own browser history on your device, and you can clear it at any time. Nothing is sent to a server.
